STAY INFORMED

Before You Apply

A few checks can help you make a more informed decision.

01

Verify the company

Find its official website independently and check its identity.

02

Check the vacancy

Look for the same opening on the company's official careers page.

03

Verify the recruiter

Confirm the recruiter's identity using independently obtained contact details.

04

Protect your information

Never share passwords, OTPs or banking credentials with recruiters.

Important: TrustHire performs a preliminary text-based review. It does not independently verify companies, recruiters or vacancies. A report with no detected warnings does not guarantee that an opportunity is genuine.
